1. ISKCON Mayapur, West Bengal

ISKCON Mayapur is one of the most famous spiritual destination in India. It is located in Mayapur, West Bengal. It is the international headquarters of the International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON), founded by His Divine Grace A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Srila Prabhupada. It is especially famous for its beautiful temples, spiritual atmosphere, devotional activities and the teachings of Lord Sri Krishna.

Things to Explore

  • Visit the beautiful Temple of the Vedic Planetarium (TOVP)
  • Darshan of Sri Sri Radha Madhav and Ashth Sakhiyan
  • Participate in Hare Krishna Kirtan and escatic dance
  • Attend Bhagavad Gita and Srimad Bhagavatam classes
  • Enjoy Krishna Prasadam and serve to devotees
  • Explore the spiritual and cultural heritage of Mayapur

Learn more: Official ISKCON Mayapur Website

2. Taj Mahal, Agra

The Taj Mahal is one of the most beautiful monuments in India. It is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh. It is famous for its white marble architecture and beautiful gardens.

Things to Explore

  • Beautiful Mughal architecture
  • Historical monuments
  • Photography
  • Local food and markets

3. Jaipur, Rajasthan

Jaipur is known as the Pink City of India. It is famous for its royal palaces, forts, traditional markets, and rich culture.

Popular Attractions

  • Hawa Mahal
  • Amber Fort
  • City Palace
  • Jantar Mantar
